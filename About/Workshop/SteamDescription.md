[h1]Favorite Color Dropper[/h1]

Use RimWorld's existing color eyedropper on a humanlike pawn to sample the color they actually prefer.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Start any vanilla painting or colored-floor action that shows RimWorld's eyedropper.
[*]Hold the eyedropper key ([b]Ctrl[/b] by default).
[*]Click a humanlike pawn to sample their favorite color.
[*]Hold [b]Shift[/b] while sampling to select that pawn's ideoligion color instead.
[/olist]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Extends the vanilla eyedropper instead of adding another tool or menu.
[*]Works with humanlike pawns wherever the normal color eyedropper is active.
[*]Shift sampling supports ideoligion colors when Ideology is installed and the pawn has one.
[*]If a requested color is unavailable, the current active color stays unchanged and RimWorld shows a small localized rejection message.
[*]No settings, new research, items, or save data.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Supports RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 unstable.
[*]Requires Harmony.
[*]Ideology is optional; favorite-color sampling does not require it.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
[/list]
